Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026 (replaces the version of May 8, 2026). This Policy is prepared in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation 2016/679).

Jai Tutor is an offline-first language learning app for Android and iOS. This Policy explains what personal data the app processes, for what purposes, on what legal basis, and how you can exercise your rights.

1.Who processes the data (controller)

Data controller: Roman Zatolokin, a natural person carrying out individual activity (individuali veikla) in the Republic of Lithuania; Individual Activity Certificate (Pažyma) No. 1215311. Address: Bukčių g. 4-49, LT-04127 Vilnius, Lithuania.

Contact: jaitutor.app@gmail.com.

2.Summary

  • The learning modes (Study, Guided, Free Mode, Role Play, Missions, Flashcards) work offline. Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Your voice is processed on your device by an offline speech-recognition model. Audio is not recorded, stored or sent to any server.
  • Data leaves your device only for: the optional online AI Conversation Partner, verification of your subscription, and downloading content updates.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not show advertising.

4.Who receives the data

We engage the following processors, each only to the extent required for its function:

  • Cloudflare — our server (Workers), short-term storage of counters and statistics, and content delivery (R2).
  • OpenAI — generating AI Conversation Partner replies. According to OpenAI, data sent through its API is not used to train its models and may be retained for a limited period for abuse monitoring.
  • Google and Apple — processing payments and verifying subscriptions.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

5.Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some processors (in particular Cloudflare, OpenAI, Google and Apple) may process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States. Any such transfer relies on a European Commission adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) ensuring an adequate level of protection.

6.Advertising and tracking

The app shows no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

7.How we protect the data

All data between the app and our server is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We keep server-side data to a minimum and delete it automatically after the periods stated above.

8.Children

The AI Conversation Partner is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly process personal data of children under 13 on our server. If you believe a child has used the AI Conversation Partner, contact us and we will delete the related data.

9.Your rights

In respect of your personal data you have the right to:

  • access your data and obtain a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • request erasure;
  • restrict processing or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Data stored on your device is under your control: you can delete it at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Server-side data is deleted automatically (after about 25 hours, 12 hours or 90 days, see section 3); you can also request earlier deletion by email.

The supervisory authority in Lithuania is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija, VDAI), Vilnius. Before contacting the authority, you may write to us — we will try to resolve the matter directly.

10.Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
